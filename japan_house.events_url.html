Events FAQ

What types of events does JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles host?

JAPAN HOUSE LA presents a variety of events including lectures, performances, workshops, film screenings, panel discussions, and culinary experiences, all focused on Japanese culture and innovation.

Are events at JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles in Hollywood free?

Many events are free, such as public talks and demonstrations, but some ticketed workshops and premium experiences may require registration or payment.

Where are events held within the venue?

Events are held on Levels 2 and 5 of JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles, located inside Ovation Hollywood at 6801 Hollywood Blvd., Los Angeles, CA. Select events may also take place off-site.

How can I attend a Japanese film screening at JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles?

JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles hosts premiere screenings, exclusive talks, and live drawing sessions through film festivals and curated programs, primarily at its Level 5 salon and TCL Chinese 6 Theatres within Ovation Hollywood, with select events at additional venues.

Does JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles offer wellness or cultural workshops?

Yes. Programs exploring Japanese mindfulness are often offered, featuring wellness experiences such as aromatherapy, healthy eating, Zen breathing, meditation, and more.

Are there youth-focused events at JAPAN HOUSE Los Angeles?

Yes. Programs are carefully curated to provide young students with an immersive introduction to Japanese culture. Offerings include student tours, campus visits, and design contests, among others.
