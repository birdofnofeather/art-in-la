A New Landmark in Los Angeles

From the sculptural architecture of Ma Yansong to Mia Lehrer’s evolving landscape, the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art rises from what was once an asphalt lot to become a place where art, nature, and community meet.

A Home for the Art That Connects Us

The Lucas Museum explores how narrative art shapes our world—expressing beliefs, communicating values, expanding imagination, and inviting conversation.
