Donate Now

Make a tax-deductible donation today, and you'll play a starring role in bringing opera to the stage by funding the efforts of the hundreds of artists and staff, who move us with their work.

 
Donor Benefits
From rehearsal tickets to backstage tours, supporters get great perks. Become a member of the Friends of LA Opera, Patrons of LA Opera, or Opera Council by making a tax-deductible donation today.
More Ways to Give
There are many additional ways to support LA Opera. Learn about other ways to play a starring role in bringing our stage to life!
40th Anniversary Campaign

As LA Opera begins its next chapter, you can help build on four decades of extraordinary artistry and ensure that grand opera thrives in Los Angeles for the next 40 years and beyond. Discover how you can help shape our future!


 
Opera Ball 2027

In Fall 2027, LA Opera will celebrate the opening of its 2027/28 Season with a very special gala celebration. More information to come in Summer 2027.
