Grand Performances Returns To California Plaza For Its 40th Season of Free Summer Concerts in the Heart of Downtown Los Angeles

RSVP

Free Global Performing Arts in Downtown LA Since 1987

Founded in 1986, Grand Performances is the longest running presenter of free outdoor concerts, poetry, theater and dance in downtown Los Angeles.

Grand Performances' mission is to inspire community, celebrate diversity, and unite Los Angeles through free access to global performing arts.

GP’s summer concert series has featured Stevie Wonder, Ozomatli, Seun Kuti, Ana Tijoux, Aloe Blacc, Angelique Kidjo, Vieux Farka Touré, Chucho Valdes and more.
