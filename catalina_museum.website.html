Ceramic Artist Amelia Lockwood in First Solo Museum Exhibition

Catalina Museum for Art & History will present Amelia Lockwood: Bells, on view October 17, 2026 through April 4, 2027 in the museum’s Artists' Plaza Gallery. Lockwood’s first solo museum exhibition brings together four ceramic vessels, bloom 1–4, whose intricate surfaces draw equally from Gothic architecture and natural forms. Created for the museum, the works invite visitors into a space of close looking and reflection.

Where Island Life Meets Creativity & Vision

Located in the heart of Avalon, Catalina Museum for Art & History offers the best in art and history exhibitions and is THE place for events and programs such as our annual Tamale Festival, Oktoberfest, summer movies in our outdoor amphitheater, "Art to Go" kits for kids, and more! If you are planning a weekend getaway with family or friends, a reunion, birthday party, golf getaway or fishing trip, contact us to schedule a group tour of the museum or the historic Tuna Club. Our facility is also available for weddings, quinceañeras, graduations and celebrations of all kinds.

Rent the Museum

The Catalina Museum for Art & History was designed to provide a stunning backdrop for weddings, corporate events, outdoor dinners, birthday and anniversary parties, and receptions of all types. ​The 18,000 square-foot building offers five event spaces that can be booked singularly or in combination to accommodate up to 300 people.  From intimate garden areas to spacious open-air plazas, a 100-seat amphitheater, modern architecture, and distinctive views of Catalina’s canyons and clear waters, the museum is unlike any other venue on the island. The museum’s galleries can be made available to guests attending the events.
